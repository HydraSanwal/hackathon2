# Module 2: Digital Twin (Gazebo & Unity)

<div class="theory-section">
This module introduces Digital Twin technology in robotics, focusing on simulation platforms Gazebo and Unity. A Digital Twin is a virtual representation of a physical system that can be used for testing, validation, and optimization before implementing in the real world.
</div>

## Overview

Welcome to Module 2 of "Physical AI & Humanoid Robotics: From Theory to Action". This module explores Digital Twin technology, which creates virtual replicas of physical robotic systems. We'll examine two major simulation platforms: Gazebo (widely used in robotics research) and Unity (popular in game development and increasingly in robotics).

## Learning Objectives

By the end of this module, you will be able to:
- Understand the concept and applications of Digital Twin technology in robotics
- Explain the differences between Gazebo and Unity as simulation platforms
- Create basic simulation environments in both platforms
- Implement sensor simulation and robot control in virtual environments
- Validate robot behaviors in simulation before real-world deployment

## The Role of Simulation in Robotics

Simulation plays a crucial role in robotics development by allowing:
- Safe testing of robot behaviors without physical risk
- Rapid prototyping and iteration
- Cost-effective development and debugging
- Training of AI algorithms in virtual environments
- Validation of control algorithms before real-world deployment
